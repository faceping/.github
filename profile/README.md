## FacePing

**Offline face verification for your app.** Enrol a face once, then verify it on the phone with a liveness check. No network needed: the camera never sends a face anywhere.

Built for check-in, ticketing, membership and staff apps. One native engine, written in Rust, runs on every phone and on our servers.
